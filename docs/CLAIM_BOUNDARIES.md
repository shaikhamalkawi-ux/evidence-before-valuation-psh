# Claim boundaries

This repository deliberately separates what the public evidence supports from what it does not support.

## Hatta

The current public-information result does **not** mean that Hatta:
- failed;
- has poor operational performance;
- has negative economic value; or
- cannot provide useful grid services.

It means only that the tested project-level economic claim is not identified from the admitted public information under the strict criterion used in the study.

The reported 48,222 MWh is treated as a deliberately favorable throughput stress, not as an observed count of resilience events.

## Wivenhoe

The 2025 operational-data analysis establishes that:
- a regional Actual-LOR event does not mechanically identify a positive named-unit energy target; and
- positive energy dispatch does not mechanically identify a recorded Raise-FCAS target.

The analysis does **not** infer:
- plant failure;
- physical non-availability;
- causal non-response;
- reservoir state or state of charge;
- stored-energy sufficiency;
- delivered reserve service; or
- avoided economic loss.

The Wivenhoe evidence is an operational-data attribution test, not a validation of the Hatta economic bridge.
